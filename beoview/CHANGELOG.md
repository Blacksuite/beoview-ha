# Changelog

## 0.5.0

First public release.

- Setup from your phone: open **Beoview** in the sidebar and photograph the QR code on the BWCM.01. No restart.
- Doorbell event, ringing and call-state sensors, door snapshot, answer / hang up / unlock buttons (MQTT discovery).
- Dashboard card installed and registered automatically; works with just `type: custom:beoview-camera`.
- Live video, two-way talk, open door.
- Quick replies: upload an audio file in the Beoview panel; it is converted, named after the file, and appears in the card. Dutch replies built in.
- Blueprints: push to one or more phones with Answer / Decline / Open door; cast the card to a Google display on a ring.
